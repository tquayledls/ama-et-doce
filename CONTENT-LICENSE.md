## What is not covered

Third-party material keeps its own terms and is not relicensed here:

- **Paintings and images** in `saints/images/` come from Wikimedia Commons. Each image's
  author, source and license are on its Wikimedia Commons file page.
- **Summa Theologiae** text is the English Dominican translation (2nd rev. ed., 1920, public
  domain) as published by New Advent.
- **Quoted excerpts** from saints and Church Fathers are from the translations named in each
  page's footnotes, which are public-domain translations unless a footnote says otherwise.
- `saints/tools/vendor/` contains third-party code under its own license.
