St. Anne

Mother of the Blessed Virgin Mary, grandmother of Jesus. Memorial with St. Joachim: July 26. Today: Memorial of the Presentation of the Blessed Virgin Mary[1]

Painting

Meeting at the Golden Gate, Giotto di Bondone, Scrovegni (Arena) Chapel, Padua[2]

The Essentials

  • Name: Anne (Hebrew Hannah, "grace") is the traditional name of the mother of the Blessed Virgin Mary.[3]
  • Origin: everything we are told about the names and lives of Joachim and Anne comes from apocryphal writings: the Gospel of the Nativity of Mary, the Gospel of Pseudo-Matthew and the Protoevangelium of James. The earliest form of the last goes back to about A.D. 150, but its statements cannot be accepted as beyond doubt on its sole authority.[4] In that account the couple lived in Nazareth, rich, pious and childless.[5]
  • Born / Died: not known. No dates or places are recorded by any reliable source; the sources above are legendary.[4]
  • Feast: July 26 in the Latin Church (July 25 in the East). Her feast reached the whole Latin Church in 1584.[6] Today, November 21, the Church keeps the Presentation of the Blessed Virgin Mary, a story that the same ancient book tells of Anne and Joachim.[1] [7]
  • Patronage: Brittany; the province of Quebec; women in labour; miners.[8]

Her Story in Brief

  • Joachim offered sacrifice at a feast and was turned away by a certain Ruben, on the pretext that a man without offspring was unworthy. He went off into the mountains to pray.[5]
  • Anne, learning why he stayed away, begged the Lord to take away her barrenness, promising to dedicate her child to God's service.[5]
  • An angel told her she would conceive, and that "the fruit of thy womb shall be blessed by all the world." A daughter was born and named Miriam (Mary).[5]
  • When the child was three, Joachim and Anne led her to the temple with a procession of lamps. The priest received her, saying that the Lord "has magnified your name in all generations."[9]
  • The cult of St. Anne can be traced in the East to the fourth century. In the West she was little venerated before the thirteenth century, and her feast came to England in 1378.[6]

Famous Excerpt: about Anne

From the Protoevangelium of James (ch. 2), Anne's prayer in the garden:

"O God of our fathers, bless me and hear my prayer, as You blessed the womb of Sarah, and gave her a son Isaac."[10]

1. Catechesis

Lesson: faithful prayer in barrenness and waiting. The old account shows Anne, childless and mourning, taking her sorrow to God and asking in the words of Sarah's blessing.[10] She also promises to give the child back to God.[5] Grandparents hand on the faith as well as parents do.

Exercise: read her prayer slowly and name one thing you have been waiting on. Say her words for it.

2. Apologetics

Objection: "Catholics build devotion on legends." The Church's own scholars are frank. All we know of Anne's name and life comes from apocryphal books, which "can hardly" be accepted as beyond doubt on their own authority.[4] In the West the Fathers rejected the Protoevangelium until the Golden Legend of the thirteenth century took up its contents.[4] The Church has also corrected excesses: the Holy See in 1677 condemned the error that Anne remained a virgin in conceiving Mary.[11] The Church honours Anne as the mother of Mary without resting doctrine on these stories.

3. Theology

  • The Incarnation has a family: the Church's Collect for Joachim and Anne says God gave them the grace that of them "should be born the Mother of your incarnate Son."[12]
  • Mary's dedication: today's memorial recalls, from tradition, Mary's presentation in the temple, with the priest's words that "in you, on the last of the days, the Lord will manifest His redemption."[9]

4. Devotional Prayer

Collect, Memorial of Saints Joachim and Anne (Roman Missal, July 26):[12]
O Lord, God of our Fathers, who bestowed on Saints Joachim and Anne this grace, that of them should be born the Mother of your incarnate Son, grant, through the prayers of both, that we may attain the salvation you have promised to your people. Through our Lord Jesus Christ, your Son, who lives and reigns with you in the unity of the Holy Spirit, God, for ever and ever.

Collect, Memorial of the Presentation of the Blessed Virgin Mary (Roman Missal, November 21):[13]
As we venerate the glorious memory of the most holy Virgin Mary, grant, we pray, O Lord, through her intercession, that we, too, may merit to receive from the fullness of your grace. Through our Lord Jesus Christ, your Son, who lives and reigns with you in the unity of the Holy Spirit, God, for ever and ever.

Practice: Pray either Collect once today.

Today's Takeaway

A hidden prayer of two grieving old people is remembered as the beginning of God's answer to the world: take your waiting to God, and give back what He gives.[5]

St. Anne, pray for us.
